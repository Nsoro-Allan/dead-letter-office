# Product
Dead Letter Office teaches people to spot phishing and scams by inspecting letters with a UV lamp. The spooky interface is the teaching mechanic, not decoration: clues only glow under the lamp, which trains the habit of inspecting before trusting.

- Audience: anyone who receives email, SMS or web links.
- Hackathon: Kiroween 2026, Costume Contest. Judged on impact, Kiro implementation, design, innovation and presentation.
- Hard constraints: no accounts, no network calls, no real personal data, works offline from `index.html`.
